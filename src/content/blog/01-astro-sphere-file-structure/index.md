I’ve been exploring AstroJs recently, and honestly, it’s been a breath of fresh
air. The setup is ridiculously simple. You can get a project running in
minutes. The documentation is very good. Things feel intuitive. The structure
is clean, and overall I would say that it is easy to learn. 

I really like is how flexible it is. You can plug in anything, be it React, Svelte,
Solid, even Alpine and it just works. That makes it super convenient if you’re
working across different frameworks or want to experiment without committing to
one.

Performance-wise, Astro shines for static site generation. The sites are fast,
lightweight, and SEO-friendly right out of the box. I also appreciate how it
encourages you to ship less JavaScript, which is always a win.

Overall, AstroJs is straightforward, versatile, and practical. Whether you’re
building a small blog or a more complex project, it just feels right.
